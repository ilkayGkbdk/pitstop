# Code of conduct

pitstop follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome newcomers, give and take feedback gracefully, and keep
discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

To report a problem privately, message the maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/ilkayGkbdk). Reports are handled confidentially.
